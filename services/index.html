Emergency-ready versus planned work

Emergency-ready means a call at night is reasonable: water that will not stop, sewage in a drain or a dead water heater in winter. Planned work is anything you can schedule, like a tankless upgrade or a bathroom rough-in.

Gas smells are not a plumbing call. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
