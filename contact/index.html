What to tell the dispatcher

A short, specific description gets the right plumber and the right tools to your door on the first trip.

  • Your street address and the town, since many addresses in this area share road names across towns.
  • What is happening: a leak, a backup, no hot water, a burst pipe, a running toilet.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you were able to shut off the main valve.
  • Where in the house it is happening and roughly how old the home is.
  • A callback number in case the call drops, and who will be home to give access.

If you rent, say so and give the property manager's name if you have it. If the house is for sale or under inspection, mention that too, since it affects how repairs are documented.

How to reach us in each situation

For an emergency, call (970) 708-6013. For a planned job such as a water heater swap, a repipe or a remodel rough-in, the same number books a visit, and you can call during the day to schedule.

For a question that is not urgent, you can email contact@247plumberjohnstown.com. Email is read during business hours, so a burst pipe or a sewer backup should never wait in an inbox.

There is no online form and no chat widget. The phone is the fastest way to get a person who can send help, and it keeps your address and details out of a web queue.

Gas smell or life-threatening danger

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not turn lights on or off, do not use the phone inside, and do not call a plumber first.

If someone is in danger from flooding, electrical contact or a fire, call 911 before anything else.
