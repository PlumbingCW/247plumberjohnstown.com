How to Thaw Frozen Pipes in Johnstown, CO

A frozen pipe can burst when it thaws. The first minutes are about stopping the water and applying only gentle heat.

Call (970) 708-6013

By the Johnstown 24/7 Plumbing team · Published 2026-10-13 · Updated 2026-10-13

First, do this when a pipe freezes

A frozen pipe is not a leak yet, but it can become one in minutes. Our line is open 24/7 for frozen and burst pipes, including nights and weekends.

  • Shut off the main water valve to the house. Know where it is before winter, because you will not want to search for it.
  • Open the faucet nearest the frozen section, so water and steam can leave as the ice melts.
  • Look for any wet drywall, ceiling stains or dripping from the pipe. If you see water, leave the main valve closed and call.
  • Turn up the heat in the room and open cabinet doors so warm air reaches the pipe.
  • Call (970) 708-6013 if you cannot find the frozen section, see a crack or have no water at all.

If the pipe is already leaking, skip the thawing and call. Thawing a cracked pipe turns a small leak into a flood.

Never use a flame or a torch

Do not use a propane torch, a lighter, a camp stove or any open flame on a frozen pipe. The pipe can scorch, nearby framing and insulation can catch fire, and water inside a pipe heated too fast can turn to steam and burst it.

The safe sources of heat are slow and gentle. They take longer, but they do not put your home at risk. If a method feels like it needs a flame, it is time to call a plumber instead.

Safe ways to thaw an exposed pipe

Use these only on a pipe you can see and reach, and only when there is no sign of a leak.

  1. Start with warm air.

    Run a hair dryer on low or medium along the pipe, working from the faucet toward the frozen section.

  2. Wrap with warm towels.

    Soak towels in hot water, wring them out and wrap the pipe. Replace them as they cool.

  3. Keep the tap open.

    Water should trickle once the ice begins to melt. A full flow means the blockage is clearing.

  4. Keep electricity away from water.

    Do not use a hair dryer or heater while standing in water or with a wet cord.

  5. Check for leaks.

    When water flows, look along the pipe for drips, then open the main valve slowly and look again.

If the pipe does not thaw after a reasonable effort, or if it is inside a wall, call. A hidden frozen pipe needs a plumber, not more heat.

Johnstown sits at about 4,994 feet, west of where the Big Thompson and Little Thompson rivers meet, and Colorado winters can drop cold air onto the Front Range quickly. That is why a pipe in an unheated garage, a north wall or a crawl space needs attention before a cold snap, not after.

When to stop and call

Call the moment you hear running water you cannot explain, see a bulge or crack in the pipe, or have no water at the main. Call if the frozen pipe is in a wall, a slab, or anywhere you cannot see.

Our burst pipe repair covers frozen and split lines, and a plumber can thaw and check the pipe safely. If you suspect water is running somewhere you cannot see, leak detection finds it before the damage spreads.

Prevent the next freeze

Keep the heat on at a steady temperature, even when you travel. Insulate exposed pipes in unheated spaces with foam sleeves, seal gaps where cold air enters, and disconnect garden hoses before the first hard freeze.

On the coldest nights, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet trickle on any line that has frozen before. Know where your main valve is, and make sure everyone in the house can find it.

Most homeowners policies cover sudden accidental water damage, including some burst pipe losses, but read your own policy for what yours says about freezing.
