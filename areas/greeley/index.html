Older core, newer edges: how your Greeley house type shapes the call

Greeley began as the Union Colony in 1869 under Nathan Meeker and was incorporated on November 15, 1885. The 2020 census counted 108,795 people, so the city holds everything from very old homes near the center to recent builds at the edges.

We cannot say what is under your floor from the street address, and the pipes in two neighboring houses may not match. What helps is a quick look. Gray threaded steel at the water heater means older supply plumbing, and copper or flexible plastic means newer work. Tell the dispatcher what you see so the right parts ride in the truck.

Before We Arrive: stop the damage at your Greeley property

Old house or new, the first minutes follow the same pattern.

  • Find the main water shutoff and close it fully. Apartment tenants should check the building's shutoff or contact the property manager at once.
  • Turn off the breaker for any room where water is reaching outlets, fixtures or appliances.
  • Move rugs, electronics and stored boxes off wet floors and keep everyone out of standing water.
  • Drain the system by opening a faucet on the lowest level.
  • Dial the 24/7 line and give the dispatcher your address, your ZIP and what has been leaking or backing up.

A smell of gas means you leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from a safe distance.

Hail, floods and what they teach about drains and basements

A hailstorm and flooding hit Greeley on May 28, 2024, and in September 2013 flood waters forced mandatory evacuations in some neighborhoods. Across Weld County, about 3,000 homes were affected in 2013. Evans, next door, had wastewater restrictions in that event.

For a homeowner the lesson is preparation. Check sump pumps, clear window wells and floor drains, and know where your sewer cleanout is before the next big storm. If a sewer line backs up after heavy rain, stop using water and call. Insurance rules vary, and most homeowners policies cover sudden accidental water damage but not wear-related repair, so read your own policy.
