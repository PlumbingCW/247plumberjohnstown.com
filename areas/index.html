How the service area is drawn

The service area follows the roads people actually drive, not a circle on a map, so a town can sit outside the list and still be a short trip for the plumber. Johnstown sits near Interstate 25 and U.S. Route 34, which makes it practical to reach Milliken, Mead and Greeley, while Berthoud lies to the southwest along the Little Thompson River.

Milliken and Mead are Weld County towns, Greeley is the Weld County seat, and Berthoud straddles Larimer and Weld counties. Those county lines affect which building department handles permits, so the town pages note where that matters.

Smaller places such as Elwell, Timnath, Evans, Platteville, Frederick, Dacono and Loveland are part of the regional dispatch area for urgent calls even though they do not have separate pages.
